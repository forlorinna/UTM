About this campus

Buildings, roads, paths and the campus boundary are based on OpenStreetMap. The four landmark models were adapted from the UTMKL Campus Life project. Several heights and exterior details are approximate. The ground uses public elevation tiles and smooths local terrain.

The Gaussian gallery displays two independent scans in Spark. Houseplant was captured by Marcel Padilla. Shaded park walk was reconstructed by Necessity Robotics in India. This build uses a 20 m crop.
