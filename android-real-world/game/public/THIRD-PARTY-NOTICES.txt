CAMPUS LANDMARK MODELS
Menara Razak, MJIIT, Masjid UTMKL and Residensi UTMKL are adapted from UTMKL Campus Life by forlorinna.
https://github.com/forlorinna/UTM/tree/utmkl-campus-life/assets/architecture
Original reference-built model geometry is retained. The APK aligns the model positions to the local GIS grid.
Menara Razak Wikimedia photo and MJIIT official campus photography were consulted for comparison. Those photos are not distributed.
